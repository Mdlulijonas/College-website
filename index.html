About Meela TDI

Meela Training and Development Institute is a premier accredited institution dedicated to providing quality vocational education and skills development programs.

Founded in 2005, we have established ourselves as a leading provider of occupational qualifications, learnerships, and skills programs accredited with various SETAs and the QCTO.

Our mission is to empower individuals and organizations through relevant, high-quality training solutions that address current market needs and contribute to South Africa's economic development.

  • QCTO Accreditation
  • Various SETA Accredited Programs
  • Experienced Facilitators
  • Workplace Integration

Matric Rewrite Program

Improve Your Matric Results

Our Matric Rewrite program helps students improve their National Senior Certificate results to unlock better opportunities for tertiary education and employment.

Program Features:
  • Expert tutors with extensive experience
  • Flexible study options (full-time, part-time, weekend classes)
  • Focused revision on key subject areas
  • Past exam paper practice and review
  • Individual attention and small class sizes
Subjects Offered:
  • Mathematics
  • Mathematical Literacy
  • English Home Language
  • English First Additional Language
  • Physical Science
  • Life Sciences
  • Accounting
  • Business Studies

Admission Process

How to Apply

Our application process is designed to be straightforward and accessible to all prospective students.

  1. Complete the online application form
  2. Submit required documentation (ID, qualifications)
  3. Attend an interview (if required)
  4. Receive offer letter
  5. Complete registration and payment

Funding Options
